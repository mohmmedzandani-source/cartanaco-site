Who andwhere.

Cartana LLC is a Michigan limited liability company based in Dearborn Heights, Michigan. Mohmmed Zandani is the owner.

We are an online retailer. Customers buy from us on Amazon, and Amazon stores, packs and ships the inventory through Fulfillment by Amazon. That is why orders are Prime-eligible, and why returns and delivery questions go to Amazon.

We are not a brand and we do not make anything. We resell finished products, the way the brand made and packaged them, and only products we bought from the brand or an authorized distributor.

Why we put thepaperwork first.

A brand has good reason to be careful about who resells its products. We are new, so we cannot point to years of history. What we can point to is how we work: authorized sources only, invoices on file, your pricing policy on every listing, and honest titles and images.

Everything on our page for brands is a commitment we are willing to be held to.
