How we workwith brands.

  • Authorized sourcing only

    We buy only from brands and their authorized distributors. No liquidators, no unverified sources.

  • Every invoice on file

    Every purchase has an invoice, and we keep it. Registered with an EIN and a Michigan sales tax license.

  • Your pricing, followed

    We follow MAP and each brand’s pricing, marketplace and content rules. Titles and images stay accurate.

  • Fulfilled by Amazon

    Amazon stores, packs and ships the stock and handles customer service and returns. Orders are Prime-eligible.

  • One point of contact

    One address for your price list and policies: purchasing@cartanaco.com. Card or prepay on opening orders.
