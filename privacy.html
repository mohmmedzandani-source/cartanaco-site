Privacy,in short.

This website collects nothing about you. If you email us, we read what you send.

What this website does not do

cartanaco.com has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Its fonts, pictures and video are served from the site itself.

What our host may see

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages can keep standard technical logs, such as the address of the device that asked for a page and the time. We do not use those logs to identify or follow visitors.

If you email us

Email to purchasing@cartanaco.com, info@cartanaco.com or mohmmed@cartanaco.com reaches our mailboxes. We use what you send to answer you and to keep business records, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising. Tell us if you want a message deleted.

Amazon purchases

If you buy from Cartana on Amazon, Amazon takes the order, payment and delivery details, and its own privacy notice applies. We do not receive your payment information.
