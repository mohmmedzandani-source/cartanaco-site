How an openingorder works.

  1. You send your terms.

    Price list, minimum order, MAP and marketplace policy, UPC and case pack. Distributors add proof of authorization.

  2. We read your rules.

    We agree what we will list and at what price, before anything goes live.

  3. We pay up front.

    Card or prepay on the opening order. Net terms only after we have a track record together.

  4. Amazon ships it.

    The stock goes to Amazon FBA. We keep the invoice and keep every listing inside your policy.

Questionsbrands ask.

Who is Cartana?

Cartana LLC is a Michigan limited liability company based in Dearborn Heights, Michigan. We are an online retailer that sells on Amazon. We are a young business, founded in 2025.

Where do you buy your products?

Only from brands and their authorized distributors. We do not buy from liquidators, other resellers or unverified sources.

Can you show me invoices and paperwork?

We keep the invoice for every purchase. We are registered with an EIN and a Michigan sales tax license, and our resale certificate (Form 3372) is available on request.

Will you follow our MAP and pricing policy?

Yes, on every listing. Send us the policy with your price list and we price to it.

Do you follow our marketplace and content rules?

Yes. We follow each brand’s marketplace rules and content guidelines, and our titles and images are accurate.

What condition is the stock in?

We do not list damaged, expired or tampered stock.

Do you hijack listings or sell counterfeit goods?

No. We do not hijack listings and we do not sell counterfeit products.

Who stores and ships the products?

Amazon. Our inventory goes to Fulfillment by Amazon, so storage, packing, shipping and customer service run through Amazon and orders are Prime-eligible.

How do you pay on a first order?

By card or prepayment on opening orders. We ask for net terms only after we have built a track record with you.

What do you need from us to start?

A price list, your minimum order, your MAP and marketplace policy, and product data (UPC and case pack). Distributors also send proof of brand authorization.

Which categories do you sell?

Household and cleaning, kitchen and home, pantry and grocery, personal care, and pet supplies.

How do I reach the purchasing team?

Email purchasing@cartanaco.com. For general questions, use info@cartanaco.com.
